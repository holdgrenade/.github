# Security

How Grenade protects a session, and what someone on your Wi‑Fi, a relay's operator and the holder of a lost phone can and cannot do, is on [holdgrenade.com/security](https://www.holdgrenade.com/security).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately, whichever part of Grenade it is in (the Mac daemon, an app, the relay or the website): [open a private report on `grenade-cli`](https://github.com/holdgrenade/grenade-cli/security/advisories/new). Only the maintainers can read it.

Please do not put the details of a problem that lets someone into another person's Mac, or lets them read a session, in a public issue.

Any other problem: [open an issue on `grenade-cli`](https://github.com/holdgrenade/grenade-cli/issues).
